# Website conventions

- The company name **Way to Asia** must never be translated in any locale. Preserve it in logos, copy, metadata, accessible labels and generated translations. Keep wordmarks marked `translate="no"` and retain the build-time brand checks.

- Give each tour its own photographs. Do not reuse an image across unrelated tours, galleries, articles or decorative sections. Reuse is permitted when needed to identify the same tour on its listing card and detail page. Compare asset IDs, ignoring resize parameters, when checking duplicates.
