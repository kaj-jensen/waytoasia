# Inspiration stories: editorial research

Published 5 October 2026. Articles are original editorial writing, not accounts of personal visits. Route ideas are suggestions and do not imply confirmed access or bookable inventory.

- Lijiang: UNESCO, Old Town of Lijiang — https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/811/ (settlements, waterways, trading history and Naxi architecture).
- Gyeongju: Korea Tourism Organization, Woljeonggyo Bridge — https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=57372 (historical bridge and 2018 reconstruction); Gyeongju itinerary — https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=221692 (evening context).
- Chiang Mai: Tourism Authority of Thailand, Baan Tawai — https://thai.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/บ้านถวาย (woodcarving village in Hang Dong). Photograph location: Doi Suthep, confirmed by its Unsplash source.
- Hoi An: Vietnam Tourism — https://www.vietnam.travel/things-to-do/the-best-ways-to-explore-the-ancient-town-of-hoi-an and https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/historic-hoi-an-melting-pot-of-cultures (trading heritage, architecture and Japanese Bridge).
- Bali: UNESCO, Cultural Landscape of Bali Province — https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1194/ (subak, cooperative irrigation and water temples). The story explicitly distinguishes Tegallalang from the specific UNESCO property.
- Kyoto: Japan National Tourism Organization — https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/kansai/kyoto/gion-and-higashiyama/ (Higashiyama, Gion and Kiyomizu-dera). Japan is an inspiration country only; no destination or tour page is created.
